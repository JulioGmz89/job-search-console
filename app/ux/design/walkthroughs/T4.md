### Alternative route — To review › "Check for new openings"

Walked by keyboard:
1. Starting from the h1, one Tab reaches the button. Before the check it reads "Last checked
   Sep 24 · no AI, a few seconds".
2. Press Enter. The start is announced, and focus moves to the h1.
3. On completion, the same summary box appears above the list, in place, and the done line
   is announced. One Tab from the h1 lands on "Check for new openings", and the summary is
   next in reading order.

This is the cleanest route: no stale banner, and the result is where the user acted.

### Likely wrong turns

- **P1 goes to "Companies" (the old "Sources") and uses "Check for new openings" in its
  header.**
  - The check runs, and the nav badge and Activity update.
  - Companies shows no result. Its banner keeps saying "Checking for new openings… a few
    seconds" after the check is done (WP-T4-01).
  - Every row still reads "Last worked Sep 24", although the check just reached ten of them
    (WP-T4-02).
  - Recovery: the banner's own link "See the results in To review", the "To review 4 new"
    badge, or Activity. All are one click.
- **P1 or P3 clicks "Check companies' job boards" at the bottom of Companies**, expecting a
  scan.
  - It answers "Juniper Mobility: board not found. Every other board answered." That is
    correct for the Juniper half of the goal.
  - It finds no openings and does not say so. The heading and description ("Tests every
    link above and tells you which ones need fixing") make the difference fairly clear, and
    no count of new openings appears. So the user still looks for, and finds, "Check for new
    openings" at the top of the same page.
  - Not filed separately; part of WP-T4-02's context.
- **P2 reports from the "Needs you" card without running the check.** This is possible
  (see the note at step 1). The card's "since Sep 24" makes it clear this is old news, and
  the "New since you last looked" card says "Last checked … Sep 24", which invites the
  check.
- **P3 follows "Fix Juniper Mobility" / "Fix in Companies".** Lands on Companies, where the
  Juniper row has a red "Board not found since Sep 24" badge and a "Fix" button next to it
  (M6's F-052 complaint that the fix lived on another page is resolved).

### Keyboard and announcements

- After each page change, focus moves to the h1. "Check for new openings" is two Tabs from
  the h1 on Today and one Tab on To review, and Enter starts it.
- Start and completion are both announced in the polite status region (`#announce`).
  - Start: "Started: Check for new openings · 11 companies. This takes a few seconds."
  - Done: "Done: 4 new openings · 10 companies checked · Juniper Mobility couldn't be
    reached".
  
  A screen-reader user therefore hears the full answer to the task without moving.
- On Today, when the done card replaces the "Working now" card, focus drops to the
  document (WP-T4-03). The next Tab lands on "See the results in To review" in the stale
  banner, which happens to be useful.
- The Activity panel opens with focus inside it. Escape closes it and returns focus to the
  Activity button, which then resets from "1 done" to "Activity".
- The "Options" disclosure on To review has labelled controls: "Only this company",
  "Only openings from the last … days", "Preview without saving", and "Confirm each
  posting is still live".

### End state against the success criterion

1. **The check ran.** Activity lists "Done · Check for new openings · 11 companies"
   (simulated; it would be a `scan` run with status `succeeded`), and To review shows
   "Last checked Oct 6, 09:48".
2. **The answer is on screen.** "4 new openings" and the four names are on Today and in
   To review, and "Juniper Mobility couldn't be reached (board not found)" is on both. The
   answer is shown on the screen where the user acted (Today or To review) and announced.

The criterion would be met by all three personas on the main path and both alternatives.

## Findings

### WP-T4-01: The "Checking for new openings… a few seconds" banner stays after the check has finished
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** Today and Companies › status banner above the page content (shown when the check is started from that page)
- **Personas:** P2 (gives up when "not knowing whether something worked"), P1 on the Companies route
- **Step/Q:** step 2, Q4; wrong turn "Companies", Q4
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run)
- **What goes wrong:**
  - **On Today.** The banner keeps saying "Checking… a few seconds" directly above the
    green "DONE" card, so the page says both "still running" and "done".
  - **On Companies.** No result appears on the page at all. The banner stays on
    "Checking…" indefinitely (observed after 10+ s), while the nav shows "To review 4 new"
    and "Activity · 1 done".
  - The banner disappears only when the page re-renders, for example when Activity is
    opened.
  - `ia.md` §3 "Run feedback" says completion updates the same spot.
  - **Why not 3.** The answer is still reachable in one click from the banner's own link
    "See the results in To review". Three other indicators turn "done", and the done
    line is announced.
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T4-01.png (Companies, about 8 s after starting the check)
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Open http://127.0.0.1:4420/prototypes/T4.html and click Reset prototype.
  2. Click "Companies" in the nav.
  3. Click "Check for new openings" (top right) and wait 5 s.
  4. Observe the banner still reads "Checking for new openings… a few seconds. See the
     results in To review." The rows are unchanged, while the nav shows "To review 4 new"
     and "Activity · 1 done".
  5. For the Today variant: Reset, click "Check for new openings" on Today, wait 5 s, and
     observe the "Checking…" banner above the "JUST FINISHED · DONE" card.

### WP-T4-02: After a check, every company still says "Last worked Sep 24"
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** Companies › company rows (status line under each name)
- **Personas:** P1 (verifies on the page that replaced "Sources"), P3 ("how up to date is this?")
- **Step/Q:** wrong turn "Companies", Q4
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run)
- **What goes wrong:**
  - **The dates do not change.** After the check reports "10 companies checked" (Oct 6,
    09:48), and even after "Check companies' job boards" says "Every other board answered",
    all ten working rows still read "Last worked Sep 24".
  - **Effect on trust.** This contradicts the check's own result and makes the user doubt
    the check ran, which is part of the success criterion.
  - **Effect on Juniper.** It also blurs the one useful date: Juniper's "Board not found
    since Sep 24" no longer stands out as the only stale company.
  - **Relation to M6.** This is the remaining part of F-052 ("when did a source last
    work").
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T4-02.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Open http://127.0.0.1:4420/prototypes/T4.html and click Reset prototype.
  2. On Today, click "Check for new openings" and wait for "DONE".
  3. Click "Companies" in the nav.
  4. Observe every followed company except Juniper Mobility reads "Last worked Sep 24".

## Verdict

**A first-time user completes T4 unaided, in two actions plus a few seconds' wait.**

Today puts "Check for new openings" in the first screen. The done card states "4 new
openings · 10 companies checked · Juniper Mobility couldn't be reached". The same answer,
with the four job names, is in To review and is announced. All three parts of the goal
are on the screen where the user acted.

The step most likely to slow a first-timer is the wait in step 2: the leftover "Checking…
a few seconds" banner contradicts the "Done" card. This is worse if the user started the
check from Companies, which shows no result at all (WP-T4-01). Even there, the banner's
own "See the results in To review" link, the "4 new" nav badge and Activity lead to the
answer in one click. No severity-3 or -4 issue was found.
