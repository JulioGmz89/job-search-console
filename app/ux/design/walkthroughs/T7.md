### WP-T7-01: "Update existing CVs" offers to re-draw 6 CVs in the design that fails screening, without a warning
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** My CV › Design › "Your existing CVs" › **Update existing CVs to Two-column custom (6)** and its confirm dialog

- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run)
- **What goes wrong:**
  - In the broken start state, the page says the saved design "Two-column custom" has "Screening problems". The same page still offers **Update existing CVs to Two-column custom (6)**. The section's text ("6 tailored CVs use older designs. Updating re-draws their PDFs in Two-column custom") gives no hint that this spreads a failing design.

"Older designs" suggests some of the six may have been in other designs. If so, PDFs that were readable can be replaced by ones that are not. ## Verdict

**A first-time user completes T7 unaided in 4 actions plus a few seconds of waiting.**
