- **Start state:** `T5.html` ("a few weeks in", populated), after **Reset prototype**
  clicked while on Today
- **Personas:** P1, P2, P3
- **Path:** `app/ux/design/prototypes/README.md` › T5. Today › Waiting for you › **Granite
  Cloud — Software Engineer, Payments** › Documents › **Make tailored CV** › **Write cover
  letter** › three answers › **Tone** › **Write the letter** › **Open** / **Download** for
  each. That is about 9 actions plus two waits. Two alternative entries are walked as wrong
  turns: Applications › row **Actions ▾** › **Write cover letter for Granite Cloud**, and

- **Walked** on 2026-10-06 by `heuristic-evaluator` against
  http://127.0.0.1:4420/prototypes/T5.html (Chromium via Playwright, 1707 × 876 px, light

keyboard users, because focus drops when a document finishes. 5 findings (WP-T5-01 …

- **P1 goes to "My CV", expecting the pdf command there.**

  - Design lists "Your existing CVs … 7 tailored CVs" and a preview picker that includes

    no link to the job's Documents.
  - Recovery: the top-bar **Find** with "Granite" returns a "Documents" group with
    "Granite Cloud — Software Engineer, Payments", which links to Documents. Today and
    **Applications** also work. Each is one or two clicks (WP-T5-04).
- **P1 or P3 starts from Applications and uses the row's "Actions ▾" menu.**

    "Make tailored CV again for Granite Cloud", "Write cover letter for Granite Cloud",

  - The menu items name only the company. The menu and the row both name the role, so the

## Findings

### WP-T5-01: Focus drops to the top of the page when a document finishes
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** Job page › Documents › "Tailored CV" and "Cover letter" cards (Working → Ready)
- **Personas:** keyboard and screen-reader users (any persona)
- **Step/Q:** step 2, Q4; step 5, Q4
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run), keyboard pass
- **What goes wrong:**
  - After "Make tailored CV" (or "Write the letter"), focus sits in the card, on the
    "Writing…" text.
  - When the run finishes and the card re-renders as "Ready", focus falls to the document.
    The next Tab lands on "← Applications" at the top of the page, and the page scrolls
    up to it.
  - The result's own controls ("Open", "Download") are then about 8 Tabs away. The next
    step, "Write cover letter", is about 9 Tabs away. `ia.md` §3 "Focus" asks that focus
    stay where the user acted.
  - This happens twice on this task, once per document.
  - **Why 2 and not 1 (WP-T4-03 was 1).** In T4 the first Tab reached the result. Here
    the keyboard user is sent back past the whole Summary block twice.
  - **Why not 3.** Both completions are announced with the job's name. The cards are
    unchanged in place, so a mouse user is unaffected. Focus is not trapped and no data is
    lost: text typed into the letter form during the CV run survived the CV's completion,
    with focus kept in the field.
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T5-01.png (focus ring on "← Applications" after one Tab following CV completion)
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Open http://127.0.0.1:4420/prototypes/T5.html and click Reset prototype.
  2. Click the heading "Good morning, Alex". Press Tab five times to reach "Granite Cloud
     — Software Engineer, Payments", then press Enter.
  3. Press Tab once ("Make tailored CV") and press Enter.
  4. Wait 5 s, until the card says "Ready", then press Tab.
  5. Observe the focus ring on "← Applications" at the top of the page, not in the Tailored
     CV card. Repeat with "Write cover letter" › fill the three answers › "Write the letter"
     for the same result.

## Verdict

**A first-time user completes T5 unaided, in about 9 actions plus two short waits.**

Today's "Waiting for you" names the job and says "no CV or letter". One click lands on its
Documents, where two side-by-side cards say exactly what each button makes and what it
costs. The letter's three questions match the goal's three statements, and "Direct" is
already selected. Both runs show progress and completion in the card where they were
started. Both are announced with the job's name. Both finish with **Open** and
**Download**, so every part of the success criterion is met on the screen where the user
acted.

The step most likely to slow a user is the wait in step 2 or step 5, and only for keyboard
users. Focus jumps to the top of the page when each document finishes (WP-T5-01, severity
2), and the user must Tab back down to "Open". Everyone else meets only cosmetic
inconsistencies (WP-T5-02 … WP-T5-05, severity 1). **No severity-3 or -4 issue was found.**

Evidence files: `app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T5-01.png` … `WP-T5-05.png`.
