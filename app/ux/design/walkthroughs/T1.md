### Likely wrong turns

- **P2 or P3 opens "My CV" first**, drawn by the red "add" badge. The page says "No CV yet.
  Add it in Get set up, or paste it here." but has no field (WP-T1-01). The link returns
  to Today's box, so recovery takes one click.
- **P1 opens "Companies" first.** "Follow a company" opens an inline form at the top, with
  focus in "Company name", two fields and a collapsed "More options". "Follow" confirms
  "Following Kestrel Media · Greenhouse job board found." Back on Today, step 2 shows done
  and the header says "1 step left". This is a full recovery. Escape does not close this
  inline form; "Cancel" does. That is acceptable, since it is not a dialog.
- **Anyone who clicks "Check for new openings" before following a company** gets an
  impossible result (WP-T1-02). It does not block set-up.
- **Pasting the CV into Writing rules (M6 F-042)** was not tried. The landing page's
  dedicated CV box removes the reason to look there.

## Findings

### WP-T1-01: The empty My CV page says "or paste it here" but offers nowhere to paste
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** My CV › Content, empty workspace
- **Personas:** P2, P3 (the red "add" badge makes this their likely first click); also P1
  if they look for `cv.md` there
- **Step/Q:** wrong turn before step 1 (Q2)
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run)
- **What goes wrong:** The page reads "No CV yet. Add it in Get set up, or paste it here."
  There is no text box, file control or Save button, only the link. A first-timer looks
  for the field the sentence promises before following the link.
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T1-01.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Open http://127.0.0.1:4420/prototypes/T1.html, click "Reset prototype" on Today.
  2. Click "My CV" in the top bar.
  3. Observe: only the sentence "No CV yet. Add it in Get set up, or paste it here."
- **Notes:** Once a CV exists, the same section has an editor, "Save" and "Import from a
  file". Either show that editor empty here, or drop "or paste it here".

### WP-T1-02: "Check for new openings" runs with no company followed and reports "4 new openings" from "0 companies checked"
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** Companies › header › "Check for new openings" in the empty workspace (also on
  To review and Today), then the To review summary
- **Personas:** P1, P3
- **Step/Q:** wrong turn before step 3
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run)
- **What goes wrong:**
  - The button is enabled with nothing followed.
  - Its inline message "Checking for new openings…" never changes.
  - To review then says "Last check: 4 new openings" from "0 companies checked", naming
    companies the user never followed.
  - The first result a new user sees contradicts itself. T1 is not blocked.
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T1-02.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Open T1.html and reset on Today.
  2. Click "Companies" (0 companies), then "Check for new openings", and wait 3 s.
  3. Click "See the results in To review". Observe "4 new openings" and "0 companies
     checked."
- **Notes:** `ia.md` §3 "Empty states": the action should explain itself ("Follow a
  company first") rather than run.

### WP-T1-03: "Paste your CV, or choose a file, before saving." stays after the box is filled
- **Severity:** 1
- **Where:** Today › Get set up › Add your CV
