### Likely wrong turns

- **P2 pastes the URL into the global search on Today.** "Nothing found. Try a company
  name." (WP-T2-01). The user recovers through the "Applications" nav link.
- **P2 chooses "Save for later" to "keep" the job.**
  - The confirmation names To review, and the line under the buttons said only "Check fit
    now" writes a fit report.
  - In To review, "Check fit" works, but when it finishes the item vanishes into "Already
    checked" with no outcome on the page (WP-T2-04).
  - The user recovers through Activity.
- **P1 opens "To review" to add a link.** The page has no link field (WP-T2-06). The user
  recovers through Applications.
- **Saving a link that is already in Applications** is accepted without comment
  (WP-T2-03).

### Keyboard and announcements

- After each page change, focus moves to the h1. Enter in the link field runs "Check fit
  now", and focus moves to the progress card.
- On completion, focus drops to the document (WP-T2-05).
- Job names are links, and sortable headers are buttons.
- The Activity panel opens with focus on its heading. Escape closes it and returns focus.
- Completion is announced in the polite status region.

### End state against the success criterion

Applications has row #41, "Kestrel Media — Senior Backend Engineer · 4.1 · Apply". Its page
holds the fit report and the posting link. The answer "4.1 / 5, Apply" is on screen at
step 4. The criterion would be met.

## Findings

### WP-T2-01: A job link pasted into "Find a job, company or document" is a dead end
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** Top bar › search › results page
- **Personas:** P2 (the only text box on Today), P3
- **Step/Q:** step 1, Q2
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run)
- **What goes wrong:** Searching the job URL gives "Nothing found. Try a company name."
  Searching "Kestrel Media" lists only the company. Neither page offers to check or save
  the link.
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T2-01.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Open T2.html and reset on Today.
  2. Paste `https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/kestrelmedia/jobs/4134913` into the search
     and press Enter.
  3. Observe "Nothing found" and no action.
- **Notes:** `ia.md` §1 says search matches actions. Recognising a job link and offering
  "Check fit for this link" would remove the detour.

### WP-T2-02: The job's History lists the same fit check twice
- **Severity:** 1
- **Where:** Job page › History
- **Personas:** P1, P3
- **Step/Q:** step 5, Q4
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run)
- **What goes wrong:** "Oct 6 02:19 · fit checked: 4.1 / 5" and "Oct 6 · fit checked:
  4.1 / 5" are shown for a single check, which reads as two paid checks.
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T2-02.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Check the Kestrel Media link from Applications.
  2. Open the job and scroll to History.

### WP-T2-03: "Save for later" accepts a link that is already in Applications
- **Severity:** 1
- **Where:** Applications › Add a job › Save for later
- **Personas:** P2
- **Step/Q:** wrong turn after step 4
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run)
- **What goes wrong:** After #41 exists, saving the same link says "Saved for later in To
  review" with no "already in your applications as #41".
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T2-03.png
- **Reproduction:** after WP-T2-02's steps, save the same URL again.

### WP-T2-04: "Check fit" from To review gives no result where it was clicked; the item just vanishes
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** To review › list item › Check fit
- **Personas:** P2
- **Step/Q:** wrong-turn recovery, Q4
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run)
- **What goes wrong:** When done, the item disappears into the collapsed "Already
  checked". The page never shows the fit, the recommendation or a link to the job; only
  Activity and the live region do. `ia.md` §3 "Run feedback" says done "updates the same
  spot".
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T2-04.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Save the Kestrel Media link for later.
  2. In To review, click "Check fit" on it and wait 5 s.
  3. Observe that the item is gone with no outcome on the page.

### WP-T2-05: Focus is lost when the progress card turns into the result card
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** Applications › Add a job › progress/result card
- **Personas:** keyboard and screen-reader users
- **Step/Q:** step 4, Q4 (keyboard)
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run), keyboard pass
- **What goes wrong:** When the card re-renders as "Done", focus falls to the document. The
  next Tab lands on "Tidy up", and seven Tabs reach "Open Kestrel Media". `ia.md` §3
  "Focus".
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T2-05.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Applications › Tab to the link field › type the URL › Enter.
  2. Wait for "Done", then press Tab.

### WP-T2-06: To review says links are "saved by you" but offers no way to add one
- **Severity:** 1
- **Where:** To review › header
- **Personas:** P1
- **Step/Q:** wrong turn at step 1, Q2
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run)
- **What goes wrong:** There is no link field on the page; the only place to save a link is
  Applications › Add a job.
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T2-06.png
- **Reproduction:** Open T2.html, reset, then click "To review".

## Verdict

**A first-time user completes T2 unaided, in three actions plus the wait.** Both criteria
are met on the screen where the user acted. The step most likely to slow a first-timer is
step 1: Today has no "add a job" field in this state, and a P2 who uses the global search
hits a dead end (WP-T2-01).
