- **Where:** Today › Needs you › "a fit check didn't finish" card › **Try again**; the "Working now" and "Just finished" groups

## Verdict

**A first-time user completes T8 unaided in 1 action plus a few seconds of waiting.**

Today's first card answers the task's question before the user looks for it. The card:
- names the job, "Driftwood Analytics — Data Engineer";
- says what happened in plain words: "opened the posting but stopped before writing the fit report";
- says it is usually temporary and what retrying costs;
- offers **Try again**.

The result names the same job and its new application number. Activity and To review give two more routes, each with the same explanation and the same retry. The distractor is visibly a different role with a different problem, so it does not pull users to the wrong job. If someone checks it anyway, the real failure stays flagged on every surface. All three parts of the success criterion are met on all three routes.

The step most likely to stop a first-timer is **step 2's feedback on Today** (WP-T8-01, severity 2). The card they clicked disappears, and both "Working now" and "Just finished" appear below the fold. P2 may briefly think the click dismissed the problem, until they notice **Activity · 1 working** or scroll.

The next weakest moment is answering "what went wrong" *after* the retry (WP-T8-02, severity 2): by then only the code line remains.

**No severity-3 or -4 issue was found on the paths walked.** The dark colour scheme was not checked in this session.

Evidence files: `app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T8-01.png` … `WP-T8-05.png`.
