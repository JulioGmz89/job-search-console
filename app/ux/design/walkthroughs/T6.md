## Findings

### WP-T6-04: Some postings in the evidence share a name, and a report opened from the evidence has no way back to it
- **Severity:** 1
- **Where:**
  - Skills › "Show the evidence for gRPC" › "The 22 postings asking for it, by company";
  - the application page opened from "Flagged as a gap in your fit reports".
- **Personas:** P3 (opens the evidence to check it)
- **Step/Q:** step 3, Q4 (residual); wrong turn "follow a fit-report link"
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run, second walk)
- **What goes wrong:**
  - **Identical names.**
    - The posting list shows "Brightwater Health (5): Data Engineer ↗, Backend Engineer (Go) ↗, Data Engineer ↗, …". The two "Data Engineer" links have no fit or other detail, so they cannot be told apart without opening them.
    - Ember Payments' "Software Engineer, Payments ↗" has no fit. The user's own application #14 has exactly the same title and fit 3.5 (it is a different posting, …/4111221 against …/4111508). A P3 who knows #14 may think its fit is missing from the evidence.
  - **Getting back.** A report opened from the gap list offers only "← Applications". Browser Back returns to Skills with focus on the h1 and the evidence closed, so the user has to reopen it.
  - **Why 1.** The answer is complete without these links, and the counts and fits around them are now traceable. This is the remainder of F-077.
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T6-04.png (gRPC evidence, unfiltered, after Improve; it shows the two "Data Engineer" links and the unscored Ember Payments posting)
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Open http://127.0.0.1:4420/prototypes/T6.html, reload, and click Reset prototype.
  2. Click "Skills", then "Show the evidence for gRPC".
  3. Observe "Brightwater Health (5): Data Engineer ↗, …, Data Engineer ↗" and "Ember Payments (2): …, Software Engineer, Payments ↗" with no fit.
  4. Click "Driftwood Analytics — Backend Engineer (Go) (#23, fit 3.4)". Observe the report and the only in-page link, "← Applications".
  5. Press browser Back. Observe Skills with the gRPC evidence closed.

### WP-T6-06: "Improve the analysis" drops keyboard focus when it starts
- **Severity:** 1
- **Where:** Skills › basis line › "Improve the analysis"
- **Personas:** keyboard and screen-reader users (any persona)
- **Step/Q:** wrong turn "Improve the analysis", Q4 (keyboard)
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run, second walk), keyboard pass
- **What goes wrong:**
  - **Focus is lost.** Activating the button replaces it with "Improving…" and a progress bar. The document becomes the active element.
  - **Where the next Tab goes.** After the run completes, the first Tab lands on "Learn next" at the top of main. During the run, the next Tab target was not determined.
  - **The spec.** `ia.md` §3 "Focus" asks for focus to stay where the user acted, or to move to the nearest sensible replacement, such as the progress bar or the basis line.
  - **Why 1.** Start and completion are both announced ("Started: … You can keep working." / "Done: Skills analysis improved: 66 postings now read by Claude"), and the button disappears for good on completion, so the place lost is only a few controls. It does not affect the task's answer.
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T6-06.png (after the run completed, one Tab: the focus ring is on "Learn next")
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Open http://127.0.0.1:4420/prototypes/T6.html, reload, and click Reset prototype.
  2. Click "Skills". Tab to "Improve the analysis" and press Enter.
  3. Observe that the button is replaced by "Improving…" and a progress bar, and that no element in main has focus.
  4. Wait about 10 s for "Done: Skills analysis improved…". Press Tab and observe focus on "Learn next".

## Verdict

**A first-time user completes T6 unaided, in two actions: Skills, then "Show the evidence for gRPC".**

- **Row 1.** Learn next opens by default, and row 1 reads "gRPC · Asked for in 22 postings (12 required, 10 nice to have) · Your CV: Missing".
- **Basis line.** It accounts for all 69 postings and says when they were read.
- **Evidence.** The disclosure lists the 22 postings by company, with a count per company and the fit where known, plus the user's own fit reports that flag gRPC.
- **One screen.** The skill, the count, the companies, and where the numbers come from are all on one screen.

**The step most likely to stop or mislead a user is now none of severity 2 or higher.**
- **The filter.** The earlier problem step, ticking "Only jobs I'd apply to (fit 4 and up)", is now consistent: every number, the evidence and the order use the same 8 good-fit postings, and the page says so.
- **What remains** is of severity 1: identical posting titles and losing the evidence after a report round-trip (WP-T6-04), and a focus drop on "Improve the analysis" (WP-T6-06).

**No severity-3 or -4 issue remains. T6 passes M7's acceptance bar on this walk.** Before the M8 usability test, check the success criterion in `tasks.md` against the filter, which now reorders the list (see the note under "Likely wrong turns").
