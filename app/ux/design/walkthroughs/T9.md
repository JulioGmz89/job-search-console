### WP-T9-06: Make it again in the Writing rules list sends keyboard focus to the top of the page, and again to the body when the run ends
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** My CV › Writing rules › "When do new rules apply?" › **Make it again** (any row) (`writingSection` re-render in `app/ux/design/prototypes/app.js`)
- **Personas:** keyboard and screen-reader users of every persona; P2 most (checks every step)

## Verdict

**A first-time user completes T9 unaided on the documented path: 9 actions plus one wait, as designed.** No step gets a "no" from any persona.

What changed since the first walk:
- The list under "When do new rules apply?" is now complete and counted ("Your 6 tailored CVs"). Cobalt Freight — Staff Software Engineer has its own **Make it again**. WP-T9-01 (severity 3) is fixed.

- The job page's badge reads **Working** during a run (WP-T9-04 fixed).
- The safe wrong turns are still safe: the example rules ask first, focus **Cancel** and can be undone; the Design redraw labels itself "wording unchanged".

The step most likely to slow a first-timer is still **step 4**, but much less than before. For keyboard users, focus returns to the top of the page after **Make it again** in the list and drops to the body when the run ends (WP-T9-06, **severity 2**). This is the highest open finding on T9 and should be fixed before build, but it does not block the task.

**There is no severity-3 or -4 issue on T9, so T9 meets M7's acceptance.** Open findings: WP-T9-06 (2), WP-T9-05 (1), WP-T9-07 (1). The dark colour scheme was not checked in this session.
