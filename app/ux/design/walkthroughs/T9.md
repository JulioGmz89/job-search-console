# Cognitive walkthrough (M7 re-run): T9, change the writing voice and get a fresh CV under the new rules

- **Start state:** `T9.html` ("a few weeks in", populated). The page was loaded fresh and **Reset prototype** was clicked on Today. The saved writing rules ban "spearheaded", "leveraged", "passionate about", "results-driven" and "synergy". Cobalt Freight — Staff Software Engineer (#12) already has a tailored CV ("Made Oct 4 · Standard design · your writing rules of Oct 1"). A second Cobalt Freight job with a CV exists: "Cobalt Freight — Site Reliability Engineer" (#2).
- **Personas:** P1, P2, P3
- **Path:** `app/ux/design/prototypes/README.md` › T9, about 9 steps: **My CV** › **Writing rules** › **Add a word or phrase** "seamless" › **Add** (×3 words) › under "When do new rules apply?", Cobalt Freight — Staff Software Engineer › **Make it again**. The ready card should say "your writing rules of today".

  **That path cannot be followed as written.** The list under "When do new rules apply?" shows 8 jobs, and Cobalt Freight — Staff Software Engineer is not one of them (WP-T9-01). The route actually walked was:
  1. **My CV**;
  2. **Writing rules**;
  3. add "seamless", "cutting-edge" and "robust" (Enter for the first two, the **Add** button for the third);
  4. look for Cobalt Freight — Staff Software Engineer under "When do new rules apply?" (not there);
  5. recover with **All applications** › search "Cobalt" › **Cobalt Freight — Staff Software Engineer**;
  6. **Make it again** in the Tailored CV card, then wait;
  7. read the ready card.

  That is 11 browser actions plus one wait.

  Three wrong turns were walked as well:
  - **Start from the example rules…**;
  - adding the words in **All rules (advanced)** instead of the chips;
  - trying to get the fresh CV from **My CV › Design** (**Update this CV's layout to Executive**).

  A fourth wrong turn came out of WP-T9-01: **Make it again** on the near-miss "Cobalt Freight — Site Reliability Engineer".
- **Walked** on 2026-10-06 by `heuristic-evaluator` against http://127.0.0.1:4420/prototypes/T9.html (Chromium via Playwright, about 1707 × 876 px, light scheme). Simulated CV runs finished in about 3–4 s.

  - The dialog is titled "Replace your writing rules with the example rules?". It says: "Your 5 words to avoid and your tone notes are replaced. ## Verdict

**A first-time user very probably completes T9 unaided, but the page sends them the wrong way first. About 11 actions plus one wait, against the designed 9.**

The first half is now excellent:
- **Writing rules** is easy to find, and **Words to avoid** names the job in the user's words.
- "nothing else in them changes" answers the fear of losing rules.
- Each added word shows in place and is announced.
- The destructive example-rules action asks first, focuses Cancel, and can be undone.
- Editing the full file also works.
- The Design lure says "wording unchanged" everywhere.

The end is excellent as well. The job page's **Make it again** explains what it does, shows progress in place, and ends with "your writing rules of today (3 words added)", which is the success criterion in plain words.

The step most likely to stop a first-timer is **step 4** (WP-T9-01, **severity 3**). Right after adding the words, the page says "To get a fresh CV under the new rules, make it again:" over a list that leaves out Cobalt Freight — Staff Software Engineer. In its place is another Cobalt Freight job with its own **Make it again**:
