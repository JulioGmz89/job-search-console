## Findings

### WP-T3-01: "I've sent my application" records today's date without asking, although the user applied yesterday
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** Job page › "I've sent my application"
- **Personas:** P2, P1
- **Step/Q:** alternative route to steps 2–3, Q4
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run)
- **What goes wrong:** The button sets Applied at once, "(today, Oct 6)", with no
  "When?". The status is right, but the date is silently wrong. `ia.md` §2.2: "Choosing
  Applied offers When?".
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T3-01.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. Open T3.html and reset on Today.
  2. Click "Cobalt Freight — Staff Software Engineer" in the Waiting card.
  3. Click "I've sent my application".
  4. Observe "Saved: Applied (today, Oct 6)".

### WP-T3-02: The status menu opens below the fold, showing only "Applied — today"
- **Severity:** 1
- **Where:** Applications (filtered) › row 12 › status menu
- **Personas:** mouse users at a laptop height (876 px)
- **Step/Q:** step 2, Q4
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run)
- **What goes wrong:** The menu opens downward from a row at the bottom of the first screen.
  Only its first item is visible until the user scrolls.
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T3-02.png
- **Reproduction:** Review them › click row 12's status without scrolling.

### WP-T3-03: Changing a second row removes the first row's Undo, and Activity does not keep it
- **Severity:** 1
- **Where:** Applications › inline "Saved … · Undo"; Activity panel
- **Personas:** all
- **Step/Q:** step 5, Q4
- **Method:** walkthrough (M7 re-run)
- **What goes wrong:** Only the latest row keeps Undo, and status changes are not kept in
  Activity (`ia.md` §3 says the undo stays there). Recovery is still possible through the
  row's menu.
- **Evidence:** app/ux/design/evidence/WP-T3-03.png
- **Reproduction:** Set rows 12 and 13 to Applied, then open Activity.

## Verdict

**A first-time user completes T3 unaided, in five actions.** Today names the two target
jobs before the user asks. Each change is confirmed in the row, announced, and undoable.
The most likely slip is recording today's date instead of yesterday's (WP-T3-01,
WP-T3-02). Neither affects the success criterion.
