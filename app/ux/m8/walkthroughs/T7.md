### W8-T7-01: After the design is saved, nothing says the Cobalt Freight CV is still in its old design; the re-layout button is the last thing on the page
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** My CV › Design › preview column after **Make this my design for every CV** ("Executive is now your design for every new CV."; **Lay out this CV again in Executive**); job page › Documents › Tailored CV
- **Tasks:** T7
- **Capabilities:** R-cv-style-put, R-cv-preview, R-runs-start (cv-render)
- **Method:** walkthrough (T7 step 4, Q1 and Q2)
- **Evidence:** app/ux/m8/evidence/W8-T7-01.png (Cobalt Freight — Staff Software Engineer previewed in Executive, green verdict and the save confirmation in view; the re-layout button is below the fold)
- **Reproduction:**
  1. `node app/ux/sandbox.mjs --state broken --port 4403`, open http://127.0.0.1:4403/
  2. Today › "Your CV design fails the screening check" › **Choose a design that passes**.
  3. Click the "Executive" card, then **Make this my design for every CV**.
  4. In **Preview with**, choose "Cobalt Freight — Staff Software Engineer, Oct 6".
  5. Observe: the preview shows the Cobalt CV in Executive with "Readable by screening systems", then **Make this my design for every CV** (disabled), **Update existing CVs to this design (7)** and "Executive is now your design for every new CV." Nothing says which design the Cobalt CV's file uses. **Lay out this CV again in Executive** appears only when the page is scrolled to its end.
  6. Open http://127.0.0.1:4403/#/applications/12: Tailored CV "Ready · cv-alex-rivera-cobaltfreight-012-2026-08-25.pdf · Made Oct 6, 00:23 · written before your writing rules changed (Oct 6) · Screening check: readable by screening systems", with **Open**, **Download**, **Make it again**. No design is named and no layout update is offered.
- **Notes:** The M7 design had a panel here: "The CV for Cobalt Freight — Staff Software Engineer is still in Two-column custom" with **Update this CV's layout to Executive** as the filled button. The build dropped the "still in" line, and the action became a small outlined button at the bottom. P2 previews the Cobalt CV in the new design, sees it pass, and stops: the design is saved, but the Cobalt PDF the job page hands out is still the old layout, and no screening check of a new file was made (success criteria 2 and 3 unmet). `inventory.md` lists "Update the layout to <design>" on the job page "when the design changed"; it did not appear here. Why not 3: in this sandbox the old Cobalt PDF already passes screening, so nothing unreadable gets sent, and the preview itself is the new layout. Why not 1: it is the last step of the task and the likely place a first-timer stops.

## Verdict

**P1 and P3 complete T7 unaided in about five actions; P2 completes the design change
unaided but may stop before producing the Cobalt Freight CV.** Today sends every persona to
the right page with one click; the gallery shows each design with the user's own CV and a
verdict in words; picking only previews, saving is one clearly worded button, and leaving
unsaved is guarded. The step most likely to stop a first-timer is **step 4** (W8-T7-01):
after saving, previewing the Cobalt CV in the new design with a green verdict looks like the
finished product, nothing says the CV's file is still in its old design, and **Lay out this
CV again in Executive** is the last, smallest button on the page. Those who find it get a
clear in-place result and a "readable" verdict in three places, though the job page then
wrongly says the CV was written under the current writing rules (W8-T7-03).
