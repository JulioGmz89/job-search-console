## Verdict

**A first-time user completes T3 unaided, in nine actions** (chip, then status, Applied,
Yesterday, Save for each row). The step most likely to slow a first-timer is **step 2's
date question**: "Today" is preselected, and a P2 who saves without reading records the
wrong day. The goal says "yesterday" and "Yesterday (Oct 6)" is the next line, so this is
a slip risk, not a stop.
