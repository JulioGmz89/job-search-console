## Findings

### W8-T9-01: Several words typed into "Add a word" are saved as one phrase, so none of them is actually banned
- **Severity:** 3
- **Where:** My CV › Writing rules › Words to avoid › "Add a word" › "Add"
- **Tasks:** T9
- **Capabilities:** UI-mycv-words-add, R-cv-voice-words-add
- **Method:** walkthrough (T9 step 1, Q3)
- **Evidence:** app/ux/m8/evidence/W8-T9-01.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. `node app/ux/sandbox.mjs --state populated --port 4401`, open http://127.0.0.1:4401/#/my-cv/writing
  2. Type `seamless, cutting-edge, robust` into "Add a word" and press Enter (or click "Add").
  3. Observe: a single chip "seamless, cutting-edge, robust ×" appears beside "synergy". No message, no warning, no question; the "When rules apply" list now says the CVs were written "before your rules last changed (Oct 7)", as if the change worked.
- **Notes:** The goal hands the user three words at once, and a comma list is the natural way to type them. The result looks like success: the three words are on screen in the list of "words to avoid", but the rule forbids only the exact string "seamless, cutting-edge, robust", so the next CV can still say "robust". The user cannot find out until they read a new CV. The file-based success check also passes in this state (`voice-dna.md` contains all three words), so a persona run would not catch it. Splitting on commas (or asking "Add these 3 words separately?") would remove the trap. Severity 3: likely, silent, and it defeats the task's purpose; recovery is easy once noticed.

### W8-T9-02: "Make it again" in the tailored-CV list drops keyboard focus to the page body, during and after the run
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** My CV › Writing rules › When rules apply › row "Cobalt Freight — Staff Software Engineer" › "Make it again"
- **Tasks:** T9
- **Capabilities:** UI-mycv-remake
- **Method:** walkthrough (T9 step 2, Q4)
- **Evidence:** app/ux/m8/evidence/W8-T9-02.png (the finished row; focus is not visible in a screenshot — the accessibility snapshot shows the document root as the active element 15 s and 30 s after the click)
- **Reproduction:**
  1. `node app/ux/sandbox.mjs --state populated --port 4401`, open http://127.0.0.1:4401/#/my-cv/writing
  2. Tab to "Make it again for Cobalt Freight — Staff Software Engineer" and press Enter.
  3. Observe: the button is replaced by the working card and focus goes to the document body; pressing Tab starts again from the top of the page. When the run ends the card is replaced by "✓ Made Oct 7, 00:55, under your current rules" and focus is still on the body; nothing is announced at the row.
- **Notes:** Same as M7 WP-T9-06 (severity 2), still open. A keyboard or screen-reader user loses their place in a list of seven CVs at both moments. Focus could move to the card's "Cancel" while working and to the row's "Open" when done.

## Verdict

as made under the new rules. The step most likely to stop a first-timer is **step 1**:
typing the three words as one comma-separated entry looks like success but bans none of
them (W8-T9-01). A P2 who makes that slip finishes the task believing it worked, and only
a later CV would show otherwise.
