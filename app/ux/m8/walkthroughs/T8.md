## Findings

### W8-T8-01: When the retry finishes, the card the user clicked vanishes; the result and "Open the job" appear below the fold, without focus
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** Today › Needs you › "Failed, tried again · Tried again: still working" card; "Just finished" section
- **Tasks:** T8
- **Capabilities:** R-run-retry, UI-today-just-finished, R-run-events
- **Method:** walkthrough (T8 step 2, Q4)
- **Evidence:** app/ux/m8/evidence/W8-T8-01.png (viewport right after the run ended: three Needs-you cards and **Activity · 1 done**; no result in view)
- **Reproduction:**
  1. `node app/ux/sandbox.mjs --state broken --port 4403`, open http://127.0.0.1:4403/
  2. On the "Check fit · Driftwood Analytics — Data Engineer" card, click **Try again**. Observe: the card stays and reads "Tried again: still working."
  3. Wait about 10 s without scrolling.
  4. Observe: the card is gone. The viewport shows "3 things need you", the Quarry Systems, CV design and Juniper Mobility cards, and **Activity · 1 done**. "Just finished · … Fit 4.1 / 5 · Recommendation: Apply · Open the job" is only reached by scrolling. Press Tab: focus goes to "Show me" on the first remaining card, not to **Open the job**.
- **Notes:** Same as M7 WP-T8-01 (severity 2), half closed: the start is now shown where the user clicked, the end is not. `tasks.md` expects "Tried again: that worked" → **Open the job** at the spot; that text exists, but only in the Activity panel. The first-job form now keeps its result card in place with focus on **Open the job** (see `first-job.md`, re-check); the retry card could do the same. Why not 3: a cleared failure with a green "1 done" reads as resolved, the result is announced and one scroll away, and the task is complete. Why not 1: P2's give-up condition is "not knowing whether something worked", and the thing they watched disappears.

## Verdict

**A first-time user completes T8 unaided, in one action plus an 8-second wait**, and can
say what went wrong from the card they acted on ("the assistant opened the posting but
stopped before writing the fit report"). Today answers the task before the user looks;
the distractor and the unreadable row no longer pull P2 off course. The step most likely
to slow a first-timer is **step 2's ending** (W8-T8-01): the card they were watching
vanishes and the "Fit 4.1 / 5 · Open the job" result is one scroll down. A P2 who reads
only the viewport sees the failure cleared and **Activity · 1 done**, which is enough to
believe it worked, but not enough to see the result. The retry's unannounced tailored CV
(W8-T8-03) is the one surprise P1 would object to.
