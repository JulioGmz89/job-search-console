## Findings

### W8-T2-01: A job link pasted into the top bar's search answers "Nothing matches", even when that link is an application
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** top bar › "Find a job, company or document…"
- **Tasks:** T2
- **Capabilities:** UI-shell-search, UI-pipeline-url
- **Method:** walkthrough (T2 step 1, Q2, wrong turn)
- **Evidence:** app/ux/m8/evidence/W8-T2-01.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. `node app/ux/sandbox.mjs --state populated --port 4401`, open http://127.0.0.1:4401/
  2. Paste `https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/kestrelmedia/jobs/4134913` into "Find a job, company or document…" in the top bar.
  3. Observe: the list says "Nothing matches "https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/kestrelmedia/jobs/4134913"" (with a horizontal scrollbar). Enter does nothing. There is no "Check the fit of this link" or pointer to Applications › Add a job.
  4. Do T2's expected path so the link becomes application #41, then repeat step 2: the answer is still "Nothing matches".
- **Notes:** Same as M7 WP-T2-01 (severity 2), still open. P1 "tries the fastest-looking control first", and a search box at the top of every page is it. Two gaps: the search does not match an application by its posting link, and a pasted link does not offer the obvious next step.

### W8-T2-02: To review says links can be "saved by you" but offers no way to add one, nor a pointer to where
- **Severity:** 1
- **Where:** To review › page subtitle; no add-link field on the page
- **Tasks:** T2
- **Capabilities:** UI-pipeline-add-to-inbox, R-inbox-add
- **Method:** walkthrough (T2 step 1, Q2, wrong turn for P1)
- **Evidence:** app/ux/m8/evidence/W8-T2-02.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. `node app/ux/sandbox.mjs --state populated --port 4401`, open http://127.0.0.1:4401/#/to-review
  2. Observe: "Job links waiting to be checked: found at the companies you follow, or saved by you." The page offers "Check for new openings", "Options", the last-check summary and the list of links. Nothing adds a link, and nothing says "Save a link from Applications › Add a job".
- **Notes:** Same as M7 WP-T2-06 (severity 1), still open. P1 maps their `pipeline.md` of pending links to this page and comes here first; the recovery is one click to Applications.

## Verdict

**A first-time user completes T2 unaided, in four actions** (Applications, paste, Check fit
now, Open the job), and can answer "fit 4.1 out of 5, recommendation Apply" from the
result card alone. The step most likely to slow a first-timer is **step 1**: in a
populated workspace Today has no add-a-job box, so the user must pick "Applications"
from the top bar. The goal's own words point there; the wrong turns (the top search, To
review) cost one or two clicks and do not mislead.
