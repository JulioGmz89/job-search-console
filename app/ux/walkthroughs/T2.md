# Cognitive walkthrough — T2: Add one job by URL and get it evaluated

- **Start state:** `populated`, default `--delay 8000`
- **Personas:** P1, P2, P3
- **Expected path:** `tasks.md` › Expected paths › T2 (5 steps, about 5–7 actions)
- Walked on 2026-10-04 against a fresh `populated` sandbox (port 4400). T3 was walked in
  the same sandbox first; it touches only rows 12 and 13 and does not affect this path.

Summary: 5 steps, 3 with at least one "no" (steps 2, 3, 5). 4 findings (W-T2-01 … W-T2-04).

---

## Step 1 — Paste the URL into "Paste a job posting URL…"

| Q | Answer | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | **Yes** (all) | The user has a link and wants the app to look at it; giving it the link is the obvious first move. |
| Q2 | **Yes** (all) | The field is the first thing on the page, full width, with a literal placeholder. |
| Q3 | **Yes** (all) | "Paste a job posting URL…" is exactly the user's intent. |
| Q4 | **Yes** (all) | Pasting a valid URL turns "Evaluate now" from faded to solid orange and enables "Add to inbox". |

## Step 2 — Leave "PDF if score ≥ 3.5" ticked and click "Evaluate now"

| Q | Answer | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | **Yes** (P1, P3) / **Partly** (P2) | The goal has two parts: "find out how well it fits" and "make sure it is kept with the rest". P1 knows evaluation does both. P2 may think these are two separate actions. |
| Q2 | **Yes** | The primary button sits right of the field. |
| Q3 | **Yes** (P1) / **No** (P2, P3) | "Evaluate now" matches "how well it fits". But "Add to inbox", directly beside it, reads to a job seeker as "save it to my list" — the second half of the goal. Nothing near the buttons says that evaluating also files the job in the tracker, or that the "inbox" is a to-do list on another page (only the collapsed "What happens…" help says so, in file-path terms). P2 may click "Add to inbox", see "Added to the inbox.", and believe the job is "kept" without being evaluated. The "PDF if score ≥ 3.5" box is ticked by default; P2 cannot tell what a 3.5 is, but leaving it alone does no harm. |
| Q4 | **Yes** (all) | A run panel appears at once: "Evaluate job-boards.greenhouse.io", "Running", a ticking timer, an animated bar, "Claude is working — … a full evaluation takes several minutes", and a "Cancel" button. The title names the job board's host rather than the job and the log is prompt-level detail (H-pipeline-03), but progress is unmistakable. |

### W-T2-01: "Add to inbox" sits beside "Evaluate now" and reads as "save this job", but neither evaluates nor tracks it
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** Pipeline › add-job box › "Add to inbox" (next to "Evaluate now")
- **Tasks:** T2, T8
- **Capabilities:** UI-pipeline-add-to-inbox, UI-pipeline-evaluate-now, R-inbox-add
- **Method:** walkthrough (T2 step 2, Q3)
- **Evidence:** app/ux/evidence/W-T2-01.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. `node app/ux/sandbox.mjs --state populated --port 4400`, open http://127.0.0.1:4400/
  2. Paste `https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/kestrelmedia/jobs/4134913` into "Paste a job posting URL…".
  3. Observe: two enabled buttons, "Evaluate now" and "Add to inbox", with no explanation beside them. Nothing says that "Evaluate now" also adds the job to the table, or that "Add to inbox" only parks the link on Sources › "Show the inbox".
- **Notes:** The task's wording ("make sure it is kept with the rest of your job applications") is what a P2 would actually think, and "Add to inbox" is the closer verbal match. Related to H-pipeline-09 (the inbox is not on this page) and H-pipeline-02 (the help is in engine terms). A one-line hint ("Scores the job and adds it to your list" / "Saves the link to look at later") would remove the guess.

## Verdict

**A first-time user probably completes T2, slowly.** The URL box and "Evaluate now" are
well placed and the run shows clear progress. The data criterion is met by step 2 alone.
The answer criterion ("what did the app conclude?") is where P2 struggles: "Finished"
gives no conclusion (step 3), and the report opens out of sight (step 5). The step most
likely to stop a first-timer is **step 5**: they click the row, see only a yellow
highlight, and report the score from the table without the decision or reasons — or
give up thinking the report did not open. A smaller risk is at step 2, choosing "Add to
inbox" to "keep" the job.
