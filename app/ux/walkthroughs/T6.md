### W-T6-01: The answer sits under a header that calls the data partial and offers paid "Read with Claude" as the primary action
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** Skills › coverage header › "Read 41 postings with Claude (5 sessions)", "Fetch posting text"
- **Tasks:** T6
- **Capabilities:** UI-skills-extract, UI-skills-fetch, R-skills-extract, R-skills
- **Method:** walkthrough (T6 step 1, Q4)
- **Evidence:** app/ux/evidence/W-T6-01.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. `node app/ux/sandbox.mjs --state populated --port 4400`, open http://127.0.0.1:4400/
  2. Click "Skills". Expand "How this page works".
  3. Observe: "30 read by Claude · 40 rules only · 3 could not be read" in the header; "Fetch posting text" and "Read 40 postings with Claude (4 sessions)" are both solid orange, before the tabs and the list. Neither the buttons nor the help say what reading costs, how long it takes, or how much the ranking would change. No date says when the numbers were last updated.
- **Notes:** Same family as H-skills-02 (coverage header is pipeline jargon) and H-skills-04 (paid run in one click). For P3, whose give-up condition is "advice with no evidence behind it", the header undermines the list right above it. A line such as "Ranking based on 71 postings; reading the rest with Claude may refine it (≈ N min, uses your plan)" would let them trust what is there.

## Verdict

**Yes — a first-time user completes T6 unaided.** "Learn next" is pre-selected, explains
itself in one sentence, and one click on the top skill gives the count and the companies.
The step most likely to stop (or rather divert) a first-timer is **step 1** for P3: the
coverage header presents the data as incomplete and invites a paid "Read with Claude" run
before the user has looked at the answer. Step 2's silent filter costs trust but not
completion.
