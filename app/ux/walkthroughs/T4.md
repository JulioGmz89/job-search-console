## Verdict

**Partly.** A first-time user will run the scan (steps 1–2 work, if "Sources" is guessed),
and will name Juniper Mobility as the problem. But they will most likely report the wrong
number of new openings. The step most likely to stop them is **step 3**: the result is a
terminal log, the bold "17 waiting" competes with "4 added", and the inbox does not mark
what is new. P1 finishes unaided; P2 and P3 finish with a wrong count.
