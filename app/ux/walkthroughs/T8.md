## Verdict

**No — a first-time user is unlikely to complete T8 unaided.** P1 may get through, since
they know failed links stay pending. The step most likely to stop a first-timer is
**step 3**: after finding the failure (itself hidden behind an unrelated "data issue"), there
is no retry and no pointer to the inbox, and the inbox does not mark the failed job. Those
who do retry from the inbox then see the item revert to "Evaluate" (step 4), which reads as a
second failure, while the app has in fact merged the result into an older application row.
