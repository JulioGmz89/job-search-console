## Verdict

**Probably yes, with doubt along the way.** Every control on the path is visible and the
two documents end up where the user would look. The step most likely to stop a
first-timer is **step 6–7**: submitting the letter throws them onto the Runs page, which
says "Queued — waiting" while the table says "Running", then "Finished" with a file path
and no way to open the result. A P2 who does not think to return to Pipeline and re-open
the row will not know whether either document exists. Secondary risks: P2/P3 not reading
"PDF" as "tailored CV" (step 2), and clicking the cover-letter button of a previously
opened, different job (step 1).
