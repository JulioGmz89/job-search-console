Offer 1 · Rejected 4 · Discarded 5 · SKIP 8 · Hired 1", a "Score" select, a filter box,
and the table sorted by "Score ↓".

| Q | Answer | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | **Yes** (P1) / **Partly** (P2, P3) | P1 knows "Evaluated" is the tracker status for "not acted on yet". P2 and P3 translate "had not done anything about yet" into "status not Applied/Interview/…"; they may instead scan the full list. That also works: the table is already sorted by score. |
| Q2 | **Yes** | The chips are prominent, with counts. |

| Q4 | **Yes** | The chip turns dark, the table shrinks to 10 rows, top row "Cobalt Freight · Staff Software Engineer · 4.2", next "Driftwood Analytics · Backend Engineer (Go) · 3.9". |

### W-T3-01: The status "Evaluated" does not read as "not acted on yet"
- **Severity:** 2
- **Where:** Pipeline › status chips › "Evaluated"; table › "Status" column
- **Tasks:** T3
- **Capabilities:** UI-pipeline-status-filter, UI-pipeline-status-cell
- **Method:** walkthrough (T3 step 1, Q3)
- **Evidence:** app/ux/evidence/W-T3-01.png
- **Reproduction:**
  1. `node app/ux/sandbox.mjs --state populated --port 4400`, open http://127.0.0.1:4400/
  2. Look at the chips and the "Status" column; click "Evaluated 10".
  3. Observe: every row in the full table has been evaluated (has a score and a "Decision"), but only 10 carry the status "Evaluated". No tooltip or legend explains that this status means "scored, not yet applied or discarded".
- **Notes:** New (no H- equivalent). The status vocabulary is upstream's (`templates/states.yml`), familiar to P1 only. For P2 it is a small but real hesitation, and "Decision: Apply" in the next column adds a second, competing "what should I do" signal.

- **Tasks:** T3
- **Capabilities:** UI-pipeline-status-cell, R-status

## Verdict

**Yes — a first-time user completes T3 unaided.** Three actions, all with visible
controls. The step most likely to stop (or rather, rattle) a first-timer is **step 2**:
the row vanishes silently and the next row slides under the pointer, so P2 may wonder
whether the change was saved — and could set the wrong row. The meaning of "Evaluated"
(step 1) costs P2 a moment but is recoverable by sorting the full list by score.
