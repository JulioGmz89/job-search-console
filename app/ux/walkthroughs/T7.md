  2. Select "Report 012 · cv-alex-rivera-cobaltfreight-012"; scroll to "Themes"; click "Executive".
  3. Observe: the preview reads "Executive" and "✓ ATS check passed · score 90/100"; the button under it now says "Save & render this CV". In T7 they combine: the user sees the outcome they wanted (new design, ATS pass), leaves, and the saved theme is still the failing one. Severity 3: a likely, silent failure on the task's main goal.

## Verdict

**Probably not, for P2 and P3; yes for P1.** The theme cards with ATS badges are the right
control and P3 will love them. The step most likely to stop a first-timer is **step 3 → 4**:
clicking an "ATS pass" theme instantly shows the new design with a green ATS check, which looks
like success; the choice is not saved, the only "unsaved" notice is out of view, and leaving
the page discards it silently (W-T7-03). Those who do click "Save & render this CV" finish, but
get a JSON log rather than a clear "saved as default · new PDF passes the ATS check".
